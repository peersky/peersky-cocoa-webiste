## Current Focus

### Peeramid Labs

As founder of [Peeramid Labs](https://peeramid.xyz), I lead research and development at the intersection of AI reasoning, blockchain security, and decentralized systems. The lab's work spans agentic AI infrastructure, protocol security research, and privacy analysis — with a focus on producing rigorous, public-interest findings rather than purely commercial output.

Selected recent work:

- **[sloppoke.me](https://sloppoke.me)** — an AI code-slop firewall linking slop indicators in public repos to real-world LLM-generated code failures.
- **Bytecode-based smart contract audit tool** — 83% precision on vulnerability triage, 2.4× detection lift over baseline on a 20-contest / 603-bug historical backtest.
- **["Mixture-of-Models: Unifying Heterogeneous Agents via N-Way Self-Evaluating Deliberation"](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18338777)** — 4× CapEx reduction or 30% accuracy boost on any base model.
- Responsible disclosure of a [critical vulnerability in **Ethereum EIP-7702**](https://blog.peeramid.xyz/discovery-of-an-ecosystem-vulnerability-in-eip-7702/).

Peeramid Labs also houses my advisory and consulting engagements across blockchain and cybersecurity projects. Full context and the rest of the lab's output live on the [Resume](/resume) page and at [blog.peeramid.xyz](https://blog.peeramid.xyz).

### Educating in Blockchain and Cybersecurity

I am a lecturer at [Invisible Garden](https://invisible.garden/), a pop-up school building the next generation of blockchain and cybersecurity experts, funded by the Ethereum Foundation, Nethermind, Scroll, and others. Over two years, I've taught 100+ students across blockchain fundamentals, smart contract security, Solidity, Zero-Knowledge proofs, and FHE (Fully Homomorphic Encryption).

### Decentralized Governance Research (GovTech)

Existing internet governance largely relies on centralized authority structures inherited from pre-digital institutions. My research and engineering work explores deterministic, community-operated alternatives.

- [Decentralised Improvement Proposals](https://dip.box) — a workspace platform for editing and maintaining EIPs that enables communities to operate their own standards via a deterministic state machine. It also serves as an analytical platform for institutions studying the impact of governance policies across protocol ecosystems.
