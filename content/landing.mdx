Notable deliveries:

#### Audio DSP & Synthesizers — Erica Synths

I designed synthesizers and DSP effects for live performances starting in 2016.

![Zen delays-medium](/ZEN_DELAYS.JPG "Zen delays")

The Zen Delay is one of my favorite projects — a tape-delay pedal with a custom DSP algorithm implementing digital filtering based on the differential equation of the delay knob's rate of change. An IIR filter design with a higher-order differential equation that would normally require a PhD in DSP to derive on paper. The DSP module was fully designed by me, including hardware and clock-level optimizations that saved 30% on BoM cost against competitors. It received numerous positive reviews from world-renowned musicians as best-in-class.
