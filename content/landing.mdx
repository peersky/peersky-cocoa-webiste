### Peeramid Labs

As founder of [Peeramid Labs](https://peeramid.xyz), I lead research and development at the intersection of AI reasoning, blockchain security, and decentralized systems. The lab's work spans agentic AI infrastructure, protocol security research, and privacy analysis — with a focus on producing rigorous, public-interest findings rather than purely commercial output.

Recent highlights:

- **[sloppoke.me](https://sloppoke.me)** — an AI code-slop firewall. Demonstrates the relationship between slop indicators in public repositories and high-profile media scandals involving LLM-generated code failures.
- **Bytecode-based smart contract audit tool** (ML, not LLM wrapper) — **83% precision** on vulnerability triage (15/18 findings validated as real security issues), with **2.4× detection lift** over random code reading on a historical contest backtest (20 contests, 603 bugs). Found real bugs at competitions.
- **Health prediction engine from longitudinal data** — designed and validated a spectral graph model for longitudinal health trajectory prediction, **R²=0.52 on held-out subjects** using delay-embedded phase-space graph construction. Proved treatment response is state-dependent (**η²=0.37, p\<0.05**) by stratifying outcomes across learned health-manifold positions — validating the core hypothesis linking graph topology to personalised medicine. Systematic 8-experiment ablation across embedding types, spectral parameters, prediction horizons and decoder architectures.
- Authored **"Mixture-of-Models: Unifying Heterogeneous Agents via N-Way Self-Evaluating Deliberation"** (arXiv:2601.16863) — a distributed GPU neural architecture for heterogeneous model orchestration achieving 4× CapEx reduction or 30% accuracy boost on any base model.
- Wrote enterprise-grade Rust orchestration implementation of NSED protocol — event-driven architecture on NATS as high-speed event bus, **70k LoC + 150k LoC of tests** including e2e and documentation. SDK open-sourced.
- Filed **6 patents across 5 families** spanning multi-agent AI coordination, zero-prior-art AI architecture, entropy-based model reputation systems, cryptographic compliance infrastructure, and hardware-bound licensing.
- Identified and responsibly disclosed a critical vulnerability in **Ethereum EIP-7702**.
