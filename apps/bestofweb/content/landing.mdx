## Current Focus & Interests

### Educating in Blockchain and Cybersecurity

I am proud to be part of [Invisible Garden](https://invisible.garden/), a pop-up school that is building a new generation of blockchain and cybersecurity experts. We are funded by Ethereum Foundation, Nethermind, Scroll, and other forward-thinking organizations and giving education on blockchain, cybersecurity, Zero-Knowledge proofs, MPC/HE (Fully Homomorphic Encryption, not HFE) and other topics.

### Building Decentralized Governance frameworks
Governance is the less evolved part of the internet, it is still using old mechanisms like power of authority similar to primal tribes of monkeys. It's time to evolve.

[Peeramid Labs](https://peeramid.xyz) and we are building following protocols:

- *World Improvement Propoisals* - WIP is a reimagined social network of "1-post-a-day", allowing everyone to submit and vote on proposals to improve our world. Membership is verified trough ePassport and ZKP KYC. Daily voting power ensures democratic participation, with your vote having stronger influence in your country's group while still participating in the global community.
- [Rankify](https://rankify.it) - Is a community bootstrapping protocol that allows you to create your own governance protocol by simply creating a community and setting some entrance criteria. It's sybil attack resistant even if your entrance criteria is as simple as a token.
- [Advanturista](https://advanturista.com) - Is advanced tourism community that is building a new way to travel the world & build your own travel agency in meanwhile.

**Life is simple, so should be governance**

![Zen delays-medium](/ZEN_DELAYS.JPG "Zen delays")

Eric Synths Zen Delay is culmination of my work with Erica Synths, it is not just my favorite delay pedal, but a piece of art, that hopefully will outlive me. Zen Delay is not just pedal, its a music instrument that people love worldwide and makes me proud and happy of young years of my career.

[This Youtube review is one of my favs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0-JZ-omg-8)

*For those who understand DSP*: The whole art of building tape delay algorithm is that you need an algorithm that implements digital filtering technique with cutoff frequency of the filter based on differential equation itself (speed of change of delay knob). This leads to IIR filter design with higher order differential equation than usual digital filter, would have been a PhD in DSP for writing this on paper.
